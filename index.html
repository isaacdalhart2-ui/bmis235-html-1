Karma Indian Cuisine

Why it's the best restaurant in Spokane

Karma serves the most authentic Indian cuisine in Spokane.

My favorite thing to order is the butter chicken with garlic naan. The chicken is super juicy, and the sauce on level 3 isn't too spicy. It has just enough heat to bring out the flavor. For dessert, get the rice pudding, and finish with a cup of their good tea.

Three reasons to go

  • Good portion sizes, so you leave full.
  • The food is very authentic.
  • You can tell the ingredients are fresh.
